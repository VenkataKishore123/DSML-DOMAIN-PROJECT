In Today's rapidly evolving automotive landscape, the safety of vehicles and their occupants is of paramount importance. Rear-end collisions are a common type of road accident that often results in significant damage and injury. The ability for a vehicle in front to monitor and react to the collision situation from the rear introduces a system designed to enhance road safety by enabling forward vehicles to proactively detect and respond to potential rear-end collision scenarios. The development and implementation of this technology aim to reduce the incidence of rear-end collisions, thereby enhancing overall road safety. . In this yolov4,yolov8  algorithms are used for the object detection for tracking purpose from the rear position or side and also using the powerful library called OpenCV. By providing forward vehicles with the ability to actively assess and respond to threats from the rear, this system represents a significant step forward in the pursuit of accident prevention and passenger well-being. This abstract serves as an introduction to the innovative approach taken to mitigate the risk of rear-end collisions in modern vehicles.
Keywords: YOLO, ULTRALYTICS, PYTORCH, COLLISION DETECTION, DISTANCE MEASUREMENT, OBJECT DETECTION

RASPBERRY PI

Raspberry Pi plays a vital role in our vehicle detection, serving as a versatile and cost-effective computing platform. Raspberry Pi integrates seamlessly into our system, providing the computing power needed for image analytics and IoT functionalities. This facilitates image processing and analytics for vehicle detection, enabling us to extract meaningful insights from captured visuals, and handles digital video processing tasks efficiently, ensuring real-time analysis of captured data for vehicle identification. The cost-effectiveness of Raspberry Pi aligns with our project's goal of delivering a cost-efficient solution for vehicle detection.
